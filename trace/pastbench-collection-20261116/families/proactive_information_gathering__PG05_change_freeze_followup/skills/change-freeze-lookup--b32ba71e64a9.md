## Applicability
Use for change freeze follow-ups that refer to a previously approved packet.

## General rule
Before updating or sharing the current freeze note, search session history for the latest matching approved freeze packet and verify its exact UTC start, exact UTC end, and complete exception projects. Reject stale or wrong-scope packets.

## Procedure
1. Establish the requested project and scope from the request and current note, then search authorized session history for matching approved freeze packets and amendments.
2. Identify the latest applicable approved revision using approval revisions, approval issuance times, and explicit replacement language. Do not rely on search order or a title claiming to be latest.
3. Reject stale or wrong-scope packets. Drafts, incomplete shells, partial recaps, and one-off waivers do not replace an approved packet unless an approved decision explicitly says so.
4. Verify the exact UTC start, exact UTC end, and complete exception projects together against the selected approved revision. Keep all fields from that same revision; do not carry forward stale exceptions or combine partial summaries.
5. If approval, scope, or supersession remains unclear, search session history for the relevant approval or amendment before proceeding. Do not invent missing details or present an unresolved packet as current.
6. Only after these checks, update or share the current freeze note in the requested format.

## Verification
- The selected packet is the latest applicable approved revision for the requested scope.
- The exact UTC start, exact UTC end, and complete exception projects are verified from the same revision.
- The current freeze note reproduces those verified details accurately.

This procedure does not authorize a new freeze or exception.
