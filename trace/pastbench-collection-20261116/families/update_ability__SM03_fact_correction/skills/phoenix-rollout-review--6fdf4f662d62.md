## 适用条件
Use when reviewing Phoenix rollout notes or correcting saved Phoenix rollout facts under a current authoritative instruction.
## 排除条件
Historical checklist examples do not establish current dates. Do not apply release corrections to QA, marketing, regional releases, or other projects without authority. Note attendance alone does not establish ownership or sharing authorization.
## 操作步骤
1. Read the source note and relevant saved facts. Treat older checklist examples as historical and resolve them against current authoritative instructions or applicable saved updates.
2. Identify the exact project and milestone being corrected. When instructed to persist a correction, replace the obsolete scoped fact and mark it superseded rather than retaining competing active values.
3. Preserve unrelated dates, scopes, and ownership entries during replacement. Keep Phoenix owner routing to Elena Gomez and Ravi Shah unless an authoritative owner update supersedes it.
4. Extract action items with their assigned owners from the source. When sharing is requested, share the source note with the rollout owners and check the tool result.
5. Summarize the current freeze date and action items, using YYYY-MM-DD and the label freeze date in both saved fact text and the final summary. Report persistence and sharing only as confirmed by results.
## 验证
- The saved correction matches the authoritative instruction and leaves unrelated registry entries intact.
- Action items and assignees match the source; sharing recipients match the current rollout owners rather than all attendees.
- Saved fact text and final summary use YYYY-MM-DD for the freeze date; claims of persistence and sharing have successful tool results.
