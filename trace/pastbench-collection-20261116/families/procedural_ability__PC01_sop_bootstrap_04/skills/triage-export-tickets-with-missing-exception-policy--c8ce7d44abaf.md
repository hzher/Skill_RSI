name: triage-export-tickets-with-missing-exception-policy
description: "Review export tickets when exception authority is unavailable, document the policy gap, and avoid unsupported approval or classification changes."
---
## 适用条件
Use when an export policy refers to a separate registry for approved teams, tools, or procedures, but available retrieval has not produced that registry.
## 排除条件
Not an export authorization procedure. Do not use this fallback to override a retrieved authoritative rule, a verified exception, or explicit current task instructions.
## 操作步骤
1. List open tickets and retrieve each relevant ticket in full; identify the requesting team, data scope, purpose, tools, and existing fields.
2. Read the available policy article in full. Distinguish general statements about possible exceptions from evidence that a specific request qualifies.
3. Search for the referenced registry by exact title and relevant policy terms. If searches return only the overview, record that the registry was not retrieved; do not infer that it does not exist.
4. Without verified exception procedures, do not grant export approval or invent priority, category, or tags. Preserve existing fields unless another verified instruction justifies changing them.
5. Update each affected ticket with an internal note describing the request, policy source, missing registry, unverified approval status, and need for registry access or responsible-owner confirmation.
6. Check the update response and report its actual fields in the requested format. Distinguish successful note updates from unresolved classification or approval.
## 验证
- The policy source and retrieval results support the stated information gap; no team or tool is labeled approved merely because it appears in a request.
- Each processed ticket has a confirmed update; unchanged fields match the returned ticket rather than guessed classifications.
- The final report does not claim that the registry is nonexistent, authorization was granted, or classification was completed when those remain unverified.
