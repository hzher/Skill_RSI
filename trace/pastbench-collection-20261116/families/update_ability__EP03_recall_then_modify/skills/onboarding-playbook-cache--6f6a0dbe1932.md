## 适用条件
Use when Operations must restore a settled onboarding playbook into a current shell and prior-session search, note listing, note retrieval, and an authoritative source are available.
## 排除条件
Do not use draft, regional, candidate, or stale cached playbooks; do not alter title, tags, status, timestamps, participants, or duration metadata; do not change content beyond the explicitly authorized update.
## 操作步骤
1. Search prior-session records for the settled onboarding playbook. Resolve the original approval against later explicit authorized revisions for the same playbook; recover the latest settled version, using saved update results as corroboration. Prefer authorized sources over drafts, regional checklists, and unrelated discussions.
2. List current notes and identify the matching approved artifact shell by title and approval/artifact-shell markers.
3. Retrieve the candidate shell and any authoritative request; distinguish restoration from a newly authorized content or SLA change. A coordination request that preserves the settled SLA does not require a new change or revert a prior authorized revision. Confirm metadata preservation.
4. Apply the approved playbook to the shell, changing only the explicitly authorized field or SLA while preserving every other step, owner, status, and metadata field.
5. Retrieve the updated shell and compare its metadata and unchanged content with the pre-update record; confirm any persisted duration uses the required Nh notation.
## 验证
- The recovered source accounts for later authorized revisions rather than stopping at the original approval; applied steps, owner, status, and any authorized SLA match that evidence.
- Only the explicitly authorized content changed; title, tags, status, creation time, participants, and duration remain unchanged.
- The final retrieval confirms the approved playbook is present in the matching current shell, the settled SLA is preserved unless a new change is authorized, and no draft or regional content was used.
