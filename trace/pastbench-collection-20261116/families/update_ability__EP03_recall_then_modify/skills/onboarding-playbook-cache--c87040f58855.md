---
name: onboarding-playbook-cache
description: "Recover the approved onboarding playbook from prior-session evidence and apply it to the matching current approved shell without changing shell metadata."
---
## 适用条件
Use when Operations must restore a settled onboarding playbook into a current shell and prior-session search, note listing, and note retrieval are available.
## 排除条件
Do not use draft, regional, candidate, or stale cached playbooks; do not alter title, tags, status, timestamps, participants, or duration metadata; do not change an approved SLA without authorization.
## 操作步骤
1. Search prior-session records for the settled onboarding playbook and prefer the explicitly approved source over drafts, regional checklists, and unrelated discussions.
2. List current notes and identify the matching approved artifact shell by title and approval/artifact-shell markers.
3. Retrieve the candidate shell and any coordination request; confirm the request permits applying the approved content and preserves existing metadata and SLA terms.
4. Replace only the shell content with the approved steps and owner/status details, preserving every existing metadata field.
5. Retrieve the updated shell and compare its metadata with the pre-update record; confirm the content contains the approved playbook and any authorized duration uses the required persisted notation.
## 验证
- The source is explicitly approved and its steps, owner, status, and SLA match the applied content.
- Only the intended shell content changed; title, tags, status, creation time, participants, and duration remain unchanged.
- The final retrieval confirms the approved playbook is present in the matching current shell and no draft or regional content was used.
