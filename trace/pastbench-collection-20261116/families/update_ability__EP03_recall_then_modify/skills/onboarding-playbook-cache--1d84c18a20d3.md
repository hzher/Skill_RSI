## 适用条件
Use when Operations must restore a settled onboarding playbook or apply an authorized update to a current shell, with prior-session search, note listing, and note retrieval available.
## 排除条件
Do not use draft, regional, candidate, proposed-rewrite, or stale cached playbooks; do not alter title, tags, status, timestamps, participants, or duration metadata; do not change content beyond the explicitly authorized restoration or update.
## 操作步骤
1. Search prior-session records for the settled onboarding playbook and prefer the explicitly approved source over drafts, regional checklists, and unrelated discussions.
2. Follow relevant session references to the approved baseline and subsequent authorized updates. Recover the resulting settled version, including saved SLA revisions; do not stop at an older approval or treat a newer proposal or cache as superseding it.
3. List current notes and identify the matching approved artifact shell by title and approval/artifact-shell markers; do not reuse a historical destination ID without checking current notes.
4. Retrieve the candidate shell and any authoritative request. Confirm whether the current instruction authorizes restoration of the settled version or a further content or SLA change, and preserve shell metadata.
5. Apply the settled approved playbook to the shell, making only any further explicitly authorized change while preserving every other step, owner, status, and metadata field.
6. Retrieve the updated shell and compare its metadata and unchanged content with the pre-update record; confirm any persisted duration uses the required Nh notation.
## 验证
- The source reflects the approved baseline plus subsequent authorized settled updates; applied steps, owner, status, and SLA match that evidence.
- Only the explicitly authorized restoration or content change occurred; title, tags, status, creation time, participants, and duration remain unchanged.
- The final retrieval confirms the settled playbook is present in the matching current shell, any authorized SLA is applied, and no draft, proposed rewrite, regional content, or stale cache was used.
