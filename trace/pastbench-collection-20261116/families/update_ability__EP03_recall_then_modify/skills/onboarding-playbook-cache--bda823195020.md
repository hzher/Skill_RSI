## 适用条件
Use when Operations must restore a settled onboarding playbook into a current shell and prior-session search, note listing, note retrieval, and an authoritative update are available.
## 排除条件
Do not use draft, regional, candidate, or stale cached playbooks; do not alter title, tags, status, timestamps, participants, or duration metadata; do not change content beyond the explicitly authorized update.
## 操作步骤
1. Search prior-session records for the settled onboarding playbook and prefer the explicitly approved source over drafts, regional checklists, and unrelated discussions.
2. List current notes and identify the matching approved artifact shell by title and approval/artifact-shell markers.
3. Retrieve the candidate shell and any authoritative request; confirm the request identifies the permitted content or SLA change and requires metadata preservation.
4. Apply the approved playbook to the shell, changing only the explicitly authorized field or SLA while preserving every other step, owner, status, and metadata field.
5. Retrieve the updated shell and compare its metadata and unchanged content with the pre-update record; confirm any persisted duration uses the required Nh notation.
## 验证
- The source is explicitly approved and the applied steps, owner, status, and any authorized SLA match the evidence.
- Only the explicitly authorized content changed; title, tags, status, creation time, participants, and duration remain unchanged.
- The final retrieval confirms the approved playbook is present in the matching current shell, the authorized SLA is applied when requested, and no draft or regional content was used.
